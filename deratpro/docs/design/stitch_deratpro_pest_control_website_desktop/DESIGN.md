## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a clinical, authoritative, and deeply reassuring presence for specialized environmental hygiene services (Deratizare, Dezinsecție, Dezinfecție). The visual language bridges advanced biocide technology and environmental safety, shedding traditional industrial pest control cliches in favor of an eco-sanitary engineering aesthetic.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** Commercial operations (HoReCa, food processing, logistics, medical centers), property administrators, and homeowners requiring immediate or recurring DDD compliance.
- **Emotional Impact:** Absolute confidence, clinical cleanliness, regulatory authority, and swift relief from acute sanitary crises.

### Style Directives
- **Modern Eco-Sanitary Precision:** Sharp layouts combined with soft, protective geometry. Crisp light surfaces represent spotless hygiene, contrasted against high-depth slate hero sections that signal technical precision and round-the-clock emergency readiness.
- **Visual Restraint:** Eliminate clutter. Use technical certification badges, micro-indicators for compliance (DSP/DSVSA accreditations), and clear visual categorization across the three core services: Rodent Control (Deratizare), Insect Control (Dezinsecție), and Pathogen Elimination (Dezinfecție).

## Colors

The palette is engineered to balance ecological health, certified biocidal efficacy, and immediate emergency responsiveness.

### Color Roles
- **Primary (`#0D5C3A` - Deep Emerald):** Represents safe biological balance, environmental compliance, and certified cleanliness. Used for core interactive elements, service highlights, active states, and brand reinforcement.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` - Dark Slate Navy):** Provides architectural depth and technical gravitas. Utilized in high-impact hero modules, dark technical cards, primary typography, and footer infrastructure.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Rapid Intervention Amber):** Dedicated to high-urgency callouts, emergency 24/7 hotline dispatch buttons, hazard classifications, and rapid response timers.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` - Sanitary Canvas):** Crisp, pure foundation for service pages and quotation flows. Complemented by `#FFFFFF` for elevated foreground cards and `#E2E8F0` for structural borders.
- **Text & Foreground Tokens:** `#0F172A` for high-contrast primary text, `#475569` for explanatory technical details, and `#94A3B8` for tertiary metadata.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes rapid scanning, clear technical specifications, and legibility under critical stress conditions (e.g., severe infestations requiring urgent phone calls).

- **Typeface Selection:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers structural geometry with human, approachable terminals. It evokes clinical precision without appearing cold or sterile.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large headlines feature tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) for punchy impact in hero units and value propositions. 
- **Body & Microcopy:** Generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensure legal, chemical, and protocol disclaimers are easily digestible across mobile devices.
- **Labels & Badges:** Uppercase styling combined with positive letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) on `label-md` and `label-sm` is reserved for certification codes (e.g., `ISO 9001`, `AVIZAT DSP`, `GRUPA PRINCIPALĂ III`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive 12-column grid designed mobile-first to ensure frictionless booking and immediate dialing from smartphones during emergencies.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column system, 1rem margin, 1rem gutter. Content stacks vertically with full-width action panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column system, 1.5rem margin, 1.25rem gutter. Service cards pair into 2-column modules.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column system, max container width of 1280px, 2rem margin, 1.5rem gutter.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Compact spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) is used to group related technical specs, such as intervention timelines, price points, and certification badges.
- Standard component breathing room is handled via `space-md` (internal padding for cards) and `space-lg` (layout gaps between distinct interface sections).
- Macro section spacing utilizes `space-xl` scaled dynamically (2.5rem on mobile up to 5rem on desktop viewports).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through subtle, clean borders combined with clinical ambient shadowing to preserve an aseptic, ultra-organized visual field.

### Layering Model
- **Level 0 (Base Surface):** `#F8FAFC` for page canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` framed with a fine structural border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements):** Quotation forms, sticky intervention triggers, and filter bars. Shadow: `0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(13, 92, 58, 0.08), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Emergency Drawers):** Overlays with backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`). Shadow: `0px 20px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.15)`.

### Dark Hero Depth Accent
In the `#0F172A` hero unit, depth is elevated using a dual-layer technique: an inner surface of `#1E293B` at 60% opacity overlaid on top of a soft green ambient radial flare (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(13, 92, 58, 0.25) 0%, transparent 70%)`).

## Shapes

The design system utilizes rounded level `2`, balancing ergonomic comfort with architectural precision.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, technical data chips, service parameter badges, and small buttons.
- **Card & Modal Radius (1rem / 16px):** Standard for content cards, protocol breakdowns, and comparative pricing tiers (`rounded-lg`).
- **Surface Enclosures (1.5rem / 24px):** Utilized for primary hero feature cards, mobile emergency bottom sheets, and floating notification modules (`rounded-xl`).
- **Pill Geometry (Full Round):** Exclusively reserved for status badges (e.g., `ACTIV`, `URGENȚĂ 24H`) and primary emergency call triggers.
